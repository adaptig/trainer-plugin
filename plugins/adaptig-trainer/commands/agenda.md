The trainer wants a pre-workshop agenda for a client who has said yes. What they gave you: $ARGUMENTS

Use the `adaptig-trainer:explainer-one-pager` skill to make one A4 page for the client and their participants. It is an agenda, not an explainer:

- **Content.** The workshop title; date, time and place, or "online", when the trainer gave them; who it is for; three to five things participants will be able to do; the day at a glance (four to six blocks, each with its time and one line on what happens); what to bring or set up beforehand (laptop, the AI tool account they will use, one real task or document to work on, anonymised); and the trainer's name and contact details.
- **Sources.** Take it from the agreed proposal, the brief, or an existing timed plan in this conversation. Skip the skill's research step: nothing here needs a web search. Never invent a date, a room, a tool or a promise the proposal does not make.
- **No prices.** Fees and terms belong in the proposal, not on the agenda.
- **The trainer's details** come from their saved details (the `trainer-details.md` reference the skill links to); if they are not saved, ask at the end of the reply, with any other question.
- **Language.** The client's language (the `languages.md` reference the skill links to); put the tag in the template's `{{LANG}}` slot.

Give the agenda text in the chat first, then the checked PDF (or the HTML when no browser works). The blocks and times here are the starting point for step 5.
