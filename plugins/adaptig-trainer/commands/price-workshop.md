The trainer wants to work out the money for a workshop. What they gave you: $ARGUMENTS

Use the `adaptig-trainer:pricing-explainer` skill. If they gave a price and a number of participants, show the margin table with their numbers: what the client pays them, the seat fee they pay Adaptig block by block, and what is left. If one of the two numbers is missing, give the short picture with one worked example and ask for it.

The price is the trainer's own; never choose it for them. A fee they pay Adaptig never goes into anything a client reads.

When they have settled a price for a real client, offer once: "Want the proposal drafted with that price? Run /adaptig-trainer:win-client."
