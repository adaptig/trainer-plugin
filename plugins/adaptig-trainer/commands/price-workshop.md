The trainer wants to work out the money for a workshop. What they gave you: $ARGUMENTS

Use the `adaptig-trainer:pricing-explainer` skill. If they gave a price and a number of participants, show the two tables with their numbers: what is left for them, and how the seat fee adds up block by block. If one of the two numbers is missing, give the short picture with one worked example as a table and ask for it. Use the currency from their saved details (the `trainer-details.md` reference the skill links to) when they have one, and still ask for the exchange rate.

The price is the trainer's own; never choose it for them. A fee they pay Adaptig never goes into anything a client reads.

When they have settled a price for a real client, end with: "Next: step 3, the proposal at that price: `/adaptig-trainer:proposal`".
