The trainer wants a proposal. What they gave you: $ARGUMENTS

Use the `adaptig-trainer:workshop-proposal` skill to turn their notes into a finished proposal the client can read. The price is the trainer's own; ask for it if they have not given it, and never invent one. If they want to know what they keep at that price, use the `adaptig-trainer:pricing-explainer` skill; that fee never goes into the proposal.

If they give you an earlier proposal with changes, revise it and open with what changed.

When it is done, give the trainer the proposal, anything they still need to fill in, and the next step ("Once they say yes, run /adaptig-trainer:prep-workshop").

Write in the trainer's voice, in the language they use with this client. Contracts are not part of this version; if they ask for one, say so and suggest they ask Adaptig.
