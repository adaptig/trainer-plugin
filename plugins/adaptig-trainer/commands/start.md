The trainer wants to go through Adaptig's step-by-step flow. What they gave you: $ARGUMENTS

These are the seven steps. Each has its own command, and each ends by naming the next one.

| Step | Command | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `/adaptig-trainer:win-client` | The message that gets you a meeting, or a follow-up when a client goes quiet |
| 2 | `/adaptig-trainer:price-workshop` | What you pay Adaptig and what you keep, as a table |
| 3 | `/adaptig-trainer:proposal` | A proposal at your price, ready to send |
| 4 | `/adaptig-trainer:agenda` | A one-page agenda the client reads before the workshop |
| 5 | `/adaptig-trainer:prep-workshop` | A timed plan first, then exercises, participant sheets and facilitator notes |
| 6 | `/adaptig-trainer:share-workshop` | A LinkedIn post with an image, and a referral ask |
| 7 | `/adaptig-trainer:feedback` | Tell Adaptig what worked and what to fix |

**They gave nothing, or you cannot tell where they are.** Show the table, then ask one
question: "Where are you with this client: no meeting yet, had the meeting, set your
price, client said yes, or workshop done?"

**You can tell.** Say "You're at step N: <name>." in one line, then do that step now with
the skill its command uses:

1. `adaptig-trainer:warm-outreach`
2. `adaptig-trainer:pricing-explainer`
3. `adaptig-trainer:workshop-proposal`
4. `adaptig-trainer:explainer-one-pager`, as a pre-workshop agenda (follow the agenda command's rules)
5. `adaptig-trainer:training-materials`, quick version first (follow the prep-workshop command's rules)
6. `adaptig-trainer:linkedin-workshop-post`
7. `adaptig-trainer:trainer-feedback`

End every step with one line naming the next: "Next: step N+1, <name>: `/adaptig-trainer:<command>`".
A trainer can skip a step or go back; follow them.
