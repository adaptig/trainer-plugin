The trainer wants to win a client. What they gave you: $ARGUMENTS

Work out where they are, from what they gave you. Ask one short question only if you cannot tell:

1. **No meeting yet.** Use the `adaptig-trainer:warm-outreach` skill to write the message that gets the meeting: through someone who already knows them, with one clear ask.
2. **Had the meeting, or have a brief.** Use the `adaptig-trainer:workshop-proposal` skill to turn their notes into a proposal the client can read. The price is the trainer's own; ask for it if they have not given it. Never invent a price. If they want to know what they keep at that price, use the `adaptig-trainer:pricing-explainer` skill.
3. **The client went quiet after a proposal.** Use the `adaptig-trainer:warm-outreach` skill to write a short follow-up.

When a step is done, give the trainer what to send, anything they still need to fill in, and the natural next step (for example, "Once they say yes, run /adaptig-trainer:prep-workshop").

Write in the trainer's voice, in the language they use with this client. Contracts are not part of this version; if they ask for one, say so and suggest they ask Adaptig.
