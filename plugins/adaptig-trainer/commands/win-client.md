The trainer wants to win a client. What they gave you: $ARGUMENTS

Work out where they are, from what they gave you. Ask one short question only if you cannot tell:

1. **No meeting yet.** Use the `adaptig-trainer:warm-outreach` skill to write the message that gets the meeting: through someone who already knows them, with one clear ask.
2. **Had the meeting, or have a brief.** That is the next steps' work. If they have not set their price, go to step 2 with the `adaptig-trainer:pricing-explainer` skill. If they have, write the proposal with the `adaptig-trainer:workshop-proposal` skill (step 3). Never invent a price.
3. **The client went quiet after a proposal.** Use the `adaptig-trainer:warm-outreach` skill to write a short follow-up.

When a step is done, give the trainer what to send and anything they still need to fill in. End with the next step in one line: "Next: step 2, price it: `/adaptig-trainer:price-workshop`" (or step 3 when the price is set).

Write in the trainer's voice, in the client's language (the `languages.md` reference the skill links to). Outreach goes out from the trainer's own account, so it needs no saved details. Contracts are not part of this version; if they ask for one, say so and suggest they ask Adaptig.
