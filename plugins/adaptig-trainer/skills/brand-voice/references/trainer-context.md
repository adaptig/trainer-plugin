# Adaptig context for trainers

A short trainer-facing summary, not the full curriculum or a current offer catalogue.

## What the company does

Adaptig's published positioning is “AI adoption that sticks”. Its materials describe four families: Spark (practical workshops), Shift (developing internal AI champions and applied use cases), Shape (leadership capability and direction), and Show (a keynote showcase). Adaptig teaches trainers to sell that whole journey, not a one-off workshop: present the step the client needs now and name what comes after it.

Do not infer today's durations, capacities, modules, fees, certification rules or availability from those labels. Source files differ in age and scope. Use the current approved material for the specific workshop and market before committing to details.

## How workshops are structured

The trainer product reference gives the spine **Knowledge → Relevance → Playbook → Exercise → Takeaway**. Preserve the approved learning goals, content and certificate requirements. Tailor the introduction, work examples, pacing and practice support only within the trainer's allowed scope; a new official curriculum requires the relevant content review.

A useful workshop output is something a participant can apply to a real task. Source material describes practical productivity and creativity use cases and tool-agnostic teaching. Start from the approved curriculum and client tools rather than building a collection of unrelated AI demos.

## Trainer role and shared brand

Existing trainer materials describe independent trainers delivering approved Adaptig workshops, with local client relationships and their own price. A trainer may sell a stage they are not yet certified to deliver, and then delivers it with a certified trainer or brings Adaptig in. A trainer does not quote Adaptig's enterprise fees or another trainer's fee, and does not promise certification outside the issuing process.

Use current approved brand assets and keep the trainer's identity clear. Do not invent a local Adaptig sub-brand.

## Still needed for production work

- The latest approved session curriculum, exercises, facilitator notes and learning requirements for the selected workshop.
- The actual client brief: participants, work tasks, starting ability, tools, constraints and desired result.
- The trainer's own fee and terms for this job, and the name that signs the offer.
- Current claim sources, reusable reference permissions and any client/participant publication permissions.

These are inputs to a specific job, not reasons to block a useful labelled first draft. Keep gaps visible; don't fill them with official-looking inventions.
