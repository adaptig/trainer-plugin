# Visual reference

Newer approved Adaptig brand guidance takes precedence; identify the revision used.

## Palette

| Colour | Hex | Typical use |
|---|---|---|
| Paper Beige | `#f9f5ea` | Main background |
| Charcoal Black | `#212124` | Text and primary logo |
| Orange Peel | `#fa5929` | Restrained accents |
| Aqua Blue | `#0a66db` | Supporting accents |
| Leaf Green | `#30966b` | Supporting accents |
| Sky Blue | `#abc9f5` | Soft background or supporting shapes |

Use one dominant background and a small number of accents. Check text contrast against
its actual background. Use Paper Beige text on Charcoal. Do not derive replacement
brand colours from screenshots or recolour supplied artwork.

## Logos and trainer identity

- Default: the genuine Charcoal wordmark on Paper Beige. Preserve its aspect ratio.
- Keep at least the height of the wordmark's **A** clear on every side; no text, image
  edge or partner mark enters that space.
- Use a solid Paper Beige plate over photographs; do not float the mark over busy content.
- Put a trainer's supplied name below the wordmark, smaller. Do not invent a local
  Adaptig sub-brand or imply an equal-weight joint venture.
- Say independent **Adaptig Certified Trainer** only when that status is confirmed.
  Never turn a trainer into Adaptig staff or company leadership in the artwork.
- Never stretch, redraw, recolour, crop or add effects to the logo. Use the genuine
  logomark for an icon/avatar where appropriate. Missing variants are not permission
  to manufacture them. Third-party marks need genuine assets and relevant permission.

## Type and slide copy

Portable templates use the bundled **Bricolage Grotesque** display face and **Figtree**
body face, with their SIL Open Font License notices. This existing Studio substitute
pairing is not a redefinition of the brand's commercial Proxima Nova type family.
Proxima Nova is not redistributed and no font licence is granted through a trainer title.

Use clear hierarchy and readable body text. Cut content before shrinking it to fit.
For client decks, use a plain descriptive heading such as “Workshop programme” or
“What participants will practise”, followed by one clear explanation or a few useful
points. Avoid turning every heading into a slogan. Use `brand-voice` for wording.

## Shapes and icons

Use the supplied original shapes sparingly to support the composition. Do not recolour
or recreate a missing asset variant. On dark backgrounds, choose a supplied light
shape; on light backgrounds, choose a supplied darker one.

The eight Lucide SVGs are general-purpose utility icons under their included ISC
licence. They can clarify a presentation, document, date, group or feedback action.
They are not Adaptig's proprietary marks. Keep stroke weight and sizing consistent;
use `currentColor` with a palette colour, retain a visible text label, and do not use
icons as unexplained decoration or certification badges.

## Before handover

Inspect the actual output, not only its source. Check clearspace, trainer identity,
type, contrast, reading order, wrapping, print boundaries, icon labels and all requested
sections. Do not invent a statistic, client proof or a credential to fill empty space.
