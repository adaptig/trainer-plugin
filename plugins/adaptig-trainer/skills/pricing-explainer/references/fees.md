# Trainer prices and fees

What a certified Adaptig trainer pays Adaptig, and the guidance trainers have been given
on what to charge. Figures are in US dollars unless a line says otherwise.

**Checked 22 November 2026.** Each figure is given as it was told to trainers, with where
and when. Adaptig can change a figure; when the platform, your trainer agreement or a
newer message from Adaptig says something else, that newer source is right and this page
is behind. Show and the education workshops are not settled yet.

## Two different kinds of money

| | Who sets it | Who pays whom |
|---|---|---|
| **Your price** | You. You are independent. | The client pays you. |
| **Adaptig's fee** | Adaptig. | You pay Adaptig for the platform, the content and the seats. |

A fee you pay Adaptig is never a client price. Do not show a seat fee or a session fee to
a client as what the training costs.

## Spark (Productivity and Creativity workshops)

### What you pay Adaptig: a seat per participant

The more people in one class, the less each seat costs. Each block of ten seats costs $10
less per seat than the block before it.

| Seats in the class | Fee per seat |
|---|---|
| 1 to 10 | $60 |
| 11 to 20 | $50 |
| 21 to 30 | $40 |
| 31 to 40 | $30 |
| 41 to 50 | $20 |
| 51 and above | $10 |

- Each block is charged at its own price; a bigger class does not make the first seats
  cheaper. Worked example: 30 participants is 10 × $60 + 10 × $50 + 10 × $40 = $1,500, an average
  of $50 a seat. (Trainer agreement, schedule of fees; repeated on the trainer calls of
  June and August 2026.)
- You are billed after the workshop, from the platform's record of who joined. A seat is
  counted when a participant uses your course link, so share the link only with the
  people who will take part. (As described to trainers in 2026.)
- When you create the course link, the platform shows the expected invoice. That is the
  figure you are billed; use it over any estimate from this table.
- A seat gives the participant one year of access to the platform.

### Seats you do not pay for

- **Three free seats every month** come with your membership. They work as a credit of
  $180 a month (three seats at the $60 price) that comes off that month's seat invoice.
  A class of three costs nothing; a class of 25 costs $1,300 − $180 = $1,120. Use the
  credit in the month or lose it; it does not carry over. (As billed in 2026.)
- **Thirty free seats after certification**, to use within 90 days of activating your
  membership (worth $1,500). Good for a first practice workshop with people you know.
- The thirty certification seats are counted as whole seats: a workshop that fits
  inside them is not billed. How they combine with a larger paid class, and which month
  a seat counts in, is worked out by the platform; the expected invoice shows the exact
  figure.

### What you charge the client

Your price is yours. Adaptig's guidance to trainers for a Spark workshop you sell
yourself is **$5,000 to $10,000** for a four-hour workshop (trainer discovery deck, April
2026; trainer call, June 2026). Price the outcome, not the hours.

## Shift (internal AI champions)

- **What you pay Adaptig:** $750 for each two-hour session. A programme is six sessions,
  so **$4,500 per client programme**, whatever you charge the client. (Trainer calls,
  June and August 2026.)
- **What you charge:** your price. The guidance given to trainers on the June 2026
  trainer call was about $30,000 for a Shift programme.
- A small client for whom the fixed fee is too much is usually better served by Spark
  split into sessions than by Shift.

## Shape (leadership)

- **What you pay Adaptig:** $375 for each one-hour session. (Trainer calls, June and
  August 2026.)
- **What you charge:** your price. The guidance given to trainers on the June 2026
  trainer call was about $10,000 for a Shape programme.

## Show and the education workshops

- **Show:** the fee was still open when this page was checked. Ask Adaptig before you
  price one.
- **Education workshops:** the trainer training has no date or price yet; Adaptig said on
  the November 2026 trainer call that both come at the next call. You cannot price these
  until you are certified for them.
