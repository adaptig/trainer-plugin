# Synthetic production example: check a staff message

Fictional practice material. Not a client record or approved Adaptig curriculum. That
note is for the trainer; participant material marks the inputs as invented and nothing more.

## Outcome and task

Create a clear staff message from supplied facts and catch unsupported additions.
Suggested practice time: twelve minutes plus a short demonstration/debrief. The trainer
must rehearse the tool route on the participant account tier before delivery.

## Source note

The fictional Example Company resource room opens on Tuesday 8 December 2026. Staff
may visit between 15:30 and 17:00 through main reception. Bring a staff pass. No booking
is needed. The source says nothing about refreshments, parking or visitor access.

## Prompt starter

> Draft a staff email of no more than 120 words using only the source note below.
> Use a clear subject and state what colleagues need to do. Do not add facts. List
> useful missing details separately as questions, not facts. [Paste the source note.]

Check each statement against the note and revise once. A successful message preserves
the day/date, visiting time, entrance, staff-pass requirement and no-booking instruction.

Beginner route: highlight those five details first and use the prompt starter.
Experienced extension: write a shorter accessible version and inspect whether a
necessary condition was lost. Ask a partner which phrase could be misread.

## Offline comparison

**A, deliberately flawed:** “Join our resource-room opening on Tuesday 8 December,
15:30–17:00. Enter through main reception. Refreshments and free parking are available.
Book your place today and bring a guest.”

**B, illustrative:** “Subject: Resource-room opening on 8 December. Staff are welcome
to visit Example Company's resource room on Tuesday 8 December 2026, between 15:30 and
17:00. Enter through main reception and bring your staff pass. No booking is needed.”

Mark supported, missing and invented statements. A invents refreshments, parking and
guest access, contradicts no booking and omits the pass. B preserves the source facts.
The paper exercise assesses source-checking; it does not establish independent AI use.

Debrief: Which error could cause a real problem? What should happen when a useful
detail is missing? Where can you apply the checking method next workday?
